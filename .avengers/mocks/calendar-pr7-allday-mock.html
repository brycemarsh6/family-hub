What I recommend (say "yes" or change any)

  1. Two all-day rows on every width (phone and iPad), then the count under the busy column. Month keeps its own rows, untouched. Why: it is your decision 9, and one rule is easier to learn on both devices.
  2. Wording: "+2" under a column in 3 Day and Week, "2 more" in Day (decision 13). Tapping either, or the arrow, opens all columns at once; the up arrow closes. Why: like Google, and one tap shows the whole week's all-day items.
  3. The arrow shows only when something is hidden (or while open, to close). A day with two or fewer all-day items shows no arrow. Why: an arrow that does nothing is a lie on screen.
  4. It closes again when you swipe to another page, as it does today. Why: the next page may have nothing hidden, so a stuck-open row would just waste space.
  5. Zoom grows text up to one and a half times. At today's zoom and below, nothing changes. Fully zoomed in, titles are 1.5 times bigger and the all-day pills grow with them. Text size still applies on top. Why: bigger than 1.5 makes Week columns on a phone show only a word or two.
  6. Long titles wrap to as many whole lines as the block holds, then end with "...". The time line shows under the title only when there is room for it, which also fixes the iPad Largest overlap on 15 minute events. Why: your answer 4, and no letter is ever cut in half.
  7. The hour labels fix shown above. Why: smallest change, keeps the columns as wide as they are now.
