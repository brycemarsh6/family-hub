Welcome steps, install help and "Finish setting up" (family copies PR 7)

Phone screens in the order a new family meets them, right after they save their recovery code on the setup page. Every question has my recommendation. None of this shows on your own copy except the new "Install on your phone" row in Settings (Q6).

Q1. Where they land, and the step order

Recommend: after "I saved my code", Continue opens Welcome instead of the dashboard. Two steps, the same dots as setup: 1. Add your family, then 2. Put Marshee on your phone. Family first, because the admin is the one holding the phone and the others can't sign in until they exist. The welcome page has no menu button and no back link, like setup, so nobody wanders off halfway.

Q2. Step 1: add your family

Recommend: heading "Who's in your family?", the admin already listed, and an "Add a person" button that opens the same Add a person sheet as Settings, Manage family (name, adult or kid, own login or profile only). Each person appears in the list as they're added. Skipping is allowed: the button reads "Next" either way, with a line saying they can add people later. Making them add someone would trap a family that wants to try it alone first.

Q5. The checklist items, how each ticks, and hiding

Recommend these five, in this order:
1. Add your family: ticks when anyone besides the admin exists. Opens Manage family. (Added because step 1 can be skipped.)
2. Pick your stores: ticks once they save the Stores screen, even if they keep the six built-in stores. Opens Settings, Stores.
3. Add pantry items: ticks at the first item in Inventory. Opens Inventory.
4. Set up a wall tablet (Optional): ticks when a Tablet login exists. Opens Settings, Manage family, where the Tablet button is.
5. Link Google Calendar (Optional): ticks when anyone links a Google account. Opens Settings, Synced calendars. Hidden entirely on a copy where Google isn't switched on.

When it goes away: when the three required items are ticked, the card turns into "You're all set" with a Done button. "Hide this" is always there, for a family that doesn't want it. Either one hides it for the whole family, for good (saved as the checklist-dismissed time). Not tied to a person, so a second admin doesn't see it again.

Never on your copy: the card only exists on a copy that was set up through a setup link (it has a "setup finished" time). Your copy never ran setup, and setup is switched off on your copy, so it can never appear for you. I'll also check the owner switch as a second lock, so a stray row can't bring it back.

Q6. Install help in Settings

Recommend: a new row "Install on your phone" in Settings, near the top with Text size and Open Marshee on, for every login (kids and the Tablet included, since it's harmless help). It opens /settings/install: the same iPhone / iPad steps as welcome step 2, with a back link to Settings and no "Go to my dashboard" button. This row is the one change you'll see on your own copy.
