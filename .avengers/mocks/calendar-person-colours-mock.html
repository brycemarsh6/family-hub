Mission 77: more vibrant person colours

Your ask: "too pastel; you should see the difference immediately in dark and light mode." Light and dark mode are side by side in every section.

My recommendation: the new colour set (section 1) with style D (solid fill, white text). Every colour keeps white text readable (at least 4.6:1), every pair is easier to tell apart than today (closest pair 19 vs 15 now), and the worst colour-blind pair is about twice as far apart as today.

1. The colours themselves

Each circle is a person's avatar with white initial. The small square is the pure colour. "White text" is the measured contrast; 4.5:1 is the minimum for readable text.

Colour-blind check

The same 8 colours as a colour-blind person sees them (simulated). "Gap" is a colour-difference score: under about 10 starts to look alike, over 20 is clearly different. The new set roughly doubles the worst gap for colour-blind viewers. Eight colours can never be perfect for everyone, so the person's initial circle stays on every card as a backup.

Normal vision
Now: closest pair purple/pink, gap 15
New: closest pair purple/pink, gap 19

Now: closest pair red/green, gap 5
New: closest pair red/amber, gap 9
Protanopia (red weak)
Now: closest pair teal/slate, gap 7
