Parent PIN, kid and tablet redeem, and wishes (R4) mock

Sample people. Rules from your plan that are already decided: one 4 digit PIN, asked on the wall tablet and on a kid's login, never on a parent's own login, a Settings switch to turn it off, and wishes with no cost until a parent sets one. The questions below are the parts the plan left open. Each has my recommendation.

Question 1: the PIN pad, and the lockout wording and numbers

My recommendation: 5 wrong tries locks PIN entry for 5 minutes, with the words below. 4 digits means 10,000 possible PINs, so 5 tries then a 5 minute wait makes guessing hopeless for a kid (it would take weeks), while a parent who mistypes twice is never stuck long. The lock is for the whole house (the tablet and every kid's login share it), and it never touches signing in. Setting a new PIN in Settings clears the lock at once.

Question 2: the PIN in Settings (parents and admins only)

My recommendation: as drawn. It sits in the Rewards section under Late checks. The switch starts On, so nothing can be redeemed by a kid or the tablet until a parent sets the PIN. Changing the PIN does not ask for the old one, because you are already signed in as a parent. You type the new PIN twice so a typo can't lock you out.

Question 3: what a kid can do on their own login

My recommendation: a kid redeems only their own rewards (with the PIN), and adds wishes only for themselves. A kid can edit or delete their own wish while it is still a wish; once a parent sets a cost it becomes a normal reward the kid can't change. A kid never sets costs, even with the PIN (a parent does that on their own phone or the tablet). Other people's rewards show their bars but no buttons.

Question 4: the wall tablet

My recommendation: with the PIN the tablet can redeem for anyone and add, edit or delete rewards (costs included), just like a parent. Without the PIN it can add wishes for anyone. Changing habit star values on the tablet waits for a later PR. Today the tablet can't open the habit editor at all (it can only tick things off, on purpose), so letting it change star values means opening habit editing on the tablet, which is a bigger change than R4. Parents still set star values on their own phones.
