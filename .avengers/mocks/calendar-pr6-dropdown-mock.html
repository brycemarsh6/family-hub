The month dropdown (PR 6 mock)

Everything here is tappable. Tap November to open and close. Swipe the little month sideways for other months, or tap a month chip. The dots are days that have something on them. Today is filled green; the day you are looking at has a green ring.

Phone

iPad, sideways (landscape)

On Schedule, Day, 3 Day and Week, tapping November opens a panel on the left and the calendar moves over. Picking a day keeps the panel open. It stays open until you tap November again, and the iPad remembers it for each person. On Month and Year only the chip row drops down (the big month is already on screen).

iPad, upright (portrait), with the side menu set to "Always open"

While the panel is open the pinned side menu squeezes to icons only, so the week keeps room for its seven days. Close the panel and the full menu comes back. Your Menu style setting itself never changes.
