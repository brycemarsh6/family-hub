My recommendation: a "Manage" button at the top of Rewards (parents and admins only), opening one page with three sections: Give or take stars, Wishes waiting for a cost, Recent. Give or take sits right on the page (no sheet) because it is the thing a parent does most. On the iPad it becomes two columns so nothing scrolls. Kids and the Tablet never see the button, and the page itself turns them away.

Question 2: give or take stars

My recommendation: pick the person (opens on the first kid), Give or Take away, the amount on the same star steps a chore uses (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 50, starting at 5), and an optional short note (up to 60 letters). One tap on the green button saves it, the bank changes at once, and it shows in Recent with an Undo (one tap, like every delete in the app). A take-away can push a bank below zero, the same as un-checking does today. With no note, history just says "Bonus stars" or "Stars taken away".

Your calls: (a) the steps above, or bigger ones too (75, 100)? (b) note optional, or required? (c) Undo on bonus stars in Recent, yes or no?

Question 3: star history, its wording and look

My recommendation: a sheet that slides up, titled with the person's name and their bank, listing the last 30 days newest first, grouped by day. Earned stars show green with a plus, spent or taken stars red with a minus. A habit only some people can see (a private habit) shows as "A private habit" to everyone else. At the bottom: "Showing the last 30 days. Older stars still count in the total." Everyone can open anyone's history (the plan already says everyone sees everyone's stars).

Question 4: where star history opens

My recommendation: on the Rewards page only, for this PR. iPad: tap a person's star bank at the top of their column. Phone: the chips already use a tap to pick a person, so a "★ (name)'s star history ›" link sits right under the chips (shown in the first phone above). The banks on Habits & Chores stay as they are, so a tap there never surprises anyone mid check-off.
