Colours here are stand ins: the real ones come from each person's own colour (the colours chat is changing them).

But you asked for a filter button, and here it is one tap deeper behind the month title, so it is easy to forget it exists. The side drawer (☰) is the whole app's menu, so a calendar filter does not belong there.

QuestionChoicesMy recommendation and why
1. Where the Filter button sitsA fourth circle (frame 1) · inside the month dropdown (frame 4) · the side drawerA fourth circle. It is the button you asked for, always in sight, the same on phone and iPad. The cost is "Nov" instead of "November" on a phone.
2. Is the filter remembered?Remembered for this login on this device · remembered for the person on every device · never rememberedRemembered for this login on this device, like the last view you picked. Hiding your work calendar on your phone then never hides it on your wife's phone or the wall tablet, and the tablet can keep its own filter all day. Frame 3's strip makes a remembered filter impossible to miss.
3. What counts as a "sub calendar"Each Google calendar under a linked accountYes: every calendar that is switched on in Settings, Calendars (any direction), listed under its Google account. Plus "Made in Marshee" for events made here.
3b. An event on more than one calendarShow it if any of its calendars is ticked · hide it if any is untickedShow it if any calendar it is on is ticked. An event made in Marshee and copied to "Forthright work" counts as both, so unticking Work only hides things that are only on Work.
3c. People and calendars togetherBoth must match · either one is enoughBoth must match: an event shows when one of its people is ticked and one of its calendars is ticked. Events pulled from Google belong to the person who linked that account. An event with nobody on it follows only the calendar boxes.
3d. Tasks and planned habitsFollow the People boxes · ignore the filterFollow the People boxes. They live only in Marshee, so the Calendars boxes do not touch them.
4. Showing a filter is onSolid circle with a count · a strip with "Show all" · bothBoth (frame 3). The circle alone is easy to miss on a busy morning; the strip says exactly what is hidden and undoes it in one tap.
5. Does Search obey the filter?Yes · noNo. Search is how you find something you cannot see, so it always looks through everything. The dashboard Calendar tile also ignores the filter (it is a quick glance at the whole family).
