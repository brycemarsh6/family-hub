Move a repeating event

Only this one can move to another day. To move every Wake Up, drop it on the same day at the new time.

Try it: press and hold any Wake Up block for half a second until it lifts, drag it up, down or sideways, and let go. Or use the shortcuts below.

The sheet: title "Move a repeating event", one line saying where it is going, then This one, All of them, Cancel. Each is 52px tall, one-thumb reach. Tapping the dark area is the same as Cancel.
This one: only that day moves. Bar: "Moved to Thu 7:30 AM", Undo.
All of them: every Wake Up on screen moves at once, Google Calendar is changed, then the bar says "Moved every Wake Up to 7:30 AM", Undo. Undo moves them all back.
Another day: "All of them" is not offered, and a grey line says why.
Cancel: the block glides back. Nothing is saved.
Google says no: nothing changes anywhere, the blocks glide back, and a yellow bar says "Couldn't change Google Calendar. Nothing moved."
