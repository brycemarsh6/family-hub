Drag to reschedule (PR 4 mock)

Press and hold an event for about half a second. It lifts: a little bigger, a shadow, an outline, and its new time where its time used to be. A small orange time also rides down the hours on the left, at the new start, like Google. Drag up or down (it snaps to 15 minutes) or across to another day. Hold near the top or bottom of the hours and they scroll. Let go: it stays, and a bar says "Moved to ..." with Undo for 5 seconds. A quick touch still scrolls, swipes or opens, as now.
