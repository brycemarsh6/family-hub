# Plans & Goals prompts (approved by Bryce, 2026-11-04)

Three kinds of wording, per life area. Once approved, this file is the
source for `src/lib/goalPrompts.ts` (PR 2) and nothing is reworded in code
without changing it here first.

- **Vision prompts**: ten per area. An empty Vision card shows **one** at a
  time, picked at random, with an "Another prompt" button that moves to the
  next one and does not repeat until all ten have been shown. Only ever one
  line of prompt text on screen, so ten costs no space. Written for a 5-, 10-
  or 15-year horizon.
- **Goal ideas**: chips on the New goal form that fill in the name.
- **Plan ideas**: chips on the Add-to-the-plan sheet that fill in the title.

Vision prompts regenerated 2026-11-04 at Bryce's request (he asked for a
Fable pass; the session cannot see its own model picker, so which model
wrote them is not recorded here). Bryce approved them as written.
