# Plans & Goals prompts (approved by Bryce, 2026-11-04; six-area revision DRAFT)

Three kinds of wording, per life area. Once approved, this file is the
source for `src/lib/goalPrompts.ts` (PR 2) and nothing is reworded in code
without changing it here first.

- **Vision prompts**: ten per area. An empty Vision card shows **one** at a
  time, picked at random, with an "Another prompt" button that moves to the
  next one and does not repeat until all ten have been shown. Only ever one
  line of prompt text on screen, so ten costs no space. Written for a 5-, 10-
  or 15-year horizon.
- **Goal ideas**: chips on the New goal form that fill in the name.
- **Plan ideas**: chips on the Add-to-the-plan sheet that fill in the title.

Vision prompts regenerated 2026-11-04 at Bryce's request (he asked for a
Fable pass; the session cannot see its own model picker, so which model
wrote them is not recorded here). Bryce approved them as written. On 2026-11-04 Bryce moved to six areas:
Financial became Work & Money (three prompts and one plan idea changed to
cover work) and Fun & Adventure was added. Those changes are a DRAFT until
Bryce approves them.

## Spiritual

Vision prompts
1. When the kids are grown, what will they say faith looked like in our home?
2. What do you want to be true about your prayers in ten years that is not true today?
3. How often do you want to be in the temple, and who do you want beside you?
4. What does a Sabbath feel like in our home in ten years?
5. Who do you want to have served long enough that it changed you?
6. What will you be able to teach the kids about God from your own experience, not from a lesson?
7. Which scripture do you want to have lived, not just read?
8. What do you want to feel when you sit down in the chapel at 45?
9. What question about God do you want to have found peace with?
10. What milestone do you want the whole family gathered for in fifteen years?

Goal ideas
- Read the Book of Mormon this year
- Temple every month
- Pray together every day

Plan ideas
- Couple prayers at bedtime
- Ten minutes of scripture before phone
- Temple on the first Saturday
- Family prayer before dinner

Plan ideas

- Meal prep on Sunday
